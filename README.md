# Cinemarium

**Cinemarium** (cinema + vivarium) is a small museum of favourite series and films that runs entirely in your browser. Each title becomes a real room from one early, iconic scene, built to scale and kept under glass, with its people still going about their day. The rooms are kept in a circular video club: walk in, step up to the case, and lean in until the room reads as a miniature.

## What's inside

- **Ten series and ten films**, one room each, chosen from a personal list, with five honourable mentions for each. The first rooms are Lumon's Macrodata Refinement floor from *Severance*, the rain-lashed hotel room of the red pill from *The Matrix*, the Swan station from *LOST*, Jerry's apartment from *Seinfeld*, Dr. Melfi's office from *The Sopranos*, Saul Goodman's office from *Breaking Bad*, Don Corleone's study from *The Godfather*, the throne room of the Red Keep from *Game of Thrones* and the dinner at the Roys' hunting lodge from *Succession*; more are under construction, and the collection is still growing.
- **The club.** A round video club, its walls lined floor to ceiling with films under a crown of light. Every visit begins at the door.
- **The case.** One room at a time on a lacquer plinth. Drag to walk around it and lean in close; switch between series and films, order them by personal rank, by year or by IMDb rating, and watch the room sink into the plinth as the next one rises. Every title has its own address.
- **Rooms that are alive.** Path-traced light baked from real fixtures, real materials, and motion-captured people who work, pause, talk and do their rounds. The terminals run, the clock on the wall keeps your time, rain runs down the windows while lightning flickers in, and down in the hatch the counter runs down from 108 minutes until someone enters the numbers.
- **Works on desktop and on your phone.** Real-time WebGPU (WebGL 2 where WebGPU is not available). It adapts its quality to the graphics it runs on, from a gaming laptop to integrated graphics and phones, and loads each room only when you visit it.
- **Free and open.** No backend, no accounts, no ads, nothing to join.

## Data & credits

Ratings come from **[IMDb](https://www.imdb.com)** through the **[OMDb API](https://www.omdbapi.com)**, and title details from **[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org)**. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

The people are built on the **[Microsoft Rocketbox](https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox)** avatar and motion-capture library (MIT). Textures, props and light come from **[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com)** and **[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com)** (CC0); the Constitution on Saul's wall is the National Archives' public-domain scan, the carpet in Don Corleone's study is a 17th-century vase carpet from The Met's open-access collection, and the stags over the Roys' fireplace are Courbet's, from the Statens Museum for Kunst. The Don's cat is a scan of a real cat by mustafasahinfb, the Iron Throne a model by The Mangle, Tyrion's, Cersei's and the Hound's heads are sculpts by kling, imthirutj and MadCatD, and the hunting lodge's trophies, armour and candelabra are scans and models by RISDNaturelab, c.turansky, soidev and AndreiVNK (all CC BY 4.0). The rooms are assembled and lit in **[Blender](https://www.blender.org)** and drawn with **[three.js](https://threejs.org)**. Full notices are in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

Please don't tap on the glass.
