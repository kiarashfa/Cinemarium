# Cinemarium

My top ten series and top ten films, each a small, realistic, living room under glass, rendered in the browser.

## How the rooms are made

Blender (headless, from Python), Cycles light baked into light maps, Rocketbox people with motion capture,
CC0 materials and props.

## Licences of assets

Recorded per asset in `pipeline/cache/MANIFEST.json` when fetched: Poly Haven and ambientCG (CC0),
Microsoft Rocketbox avatars and animations (MIT).
